Primary School Scholarship

For primary school pupils. A parent or guardian may help the child express their answers. Read the steps below, then start your application.

How to apply

  1. Submit the application form. A parent or guardian can complete it together with the child. It covers the child's school, the family's situation, and the child's goals.
  2. Send your documents if you're shortlisted. We'll contact you to ask for them.
  3. Attend an interview or assessment.
  4. Final selection and announcement.

What to have ready

With your application

  • Your school and current class or year of study
  • Your most recent academic performance
  • Details about your family and household
  • The child's personal statement (a parent or guardian may help)
  • Parent or guardian details and consent, if you're under 18

If you're shortlisted

  • Most recent school report or academic transcript
  • Proof of enrollment or admission
  • A recommendation from a teacher, school administrator, or community leader
  • Documents showing financial need, if required
