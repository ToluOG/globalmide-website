Introduction

Who We Are

The Globalmide Scholars Program is a faith-driven educational initiative dedicated to empowering underprivileged high school students with access to quality education. We believe that every child deserves a chance to learn, grow, and transform their future regardless of financial background.

Through scholarships, mentorship, and community support, Globalmide nurtures hope, opportunity, and lifelong impact.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

High school students from low-income backgrounds.

Young people showing potential, discipline, and a passion for learning.

Students committed to giving back to their communities.

Passion for innovation, sustainability, or social change.
